Preamble

We, the members of the United Students Foundation, determined to promote unity, mutual support, and academic success within our school, hereby establish these Charter Rules.

Article I: Purposes & Principles

  • Sovereign Equality: Every student member has an equal voice, regardless of grade level or background.
  • Peaceful Coexistence: Disagreements must be settled through calm discussion and mediation, never through conflict or exclusion.
  • Universal Respect: Members shall honor the rights and dignity of every classmate.

Article II: Membership & Delegations

  • Open Membership: Any student in good standing who agrees to this Charter may join as a Student Delegate.
  • Active Representation: Delegates are expected to attend General Assembly meetings and represent their classes with integrity.

Article III: The Student Security Council

  • Leadership Role: Elected student officers (President, Vice-President, Secretary) serve as the Security Council to maintain order and guide projects.
  • Resolutions: Decisions regarding major events or project funds require a majority vote by the council and approval from the General Assembly.

Article IV: Collective Action & Goodwill

  • Humanitarian Support: Members will organize student-led study groups and support initiatives to help classmates in need.
  • Sanctions: Delegates who repeatedly break school rules or cause harm to the group will face temporary suspension from foundation activities.

Article V: The General Assembly

  • Annual Summit: A full assembly of all Student Delegates shall convene at least once per term to vote on major foundation initiatives.
  • Voting Rights: Each Delegate holds one vote. Decisions shall be passed by a simple majority, ensuring every member contributes to the foundation's direction.

Article VI: Academic & Cultural Secretariat

  • Project Committees: Dedicated committees (such as Event Planning, Tutoring, and Community Outreach) shall manage specific programs under the guidance of appointed Committee Chairs.
  • Cultural Exchange: The Secretariat shall promote events that celebrate student diversity, foster creativity, and share different cultures within the school.

Article VII: Diplomatic Immunity & Safe Spaces

  • Freedom of Expression: Delegates may share ideas, ask questions, and voice concerns during assemblies without fear of judgment, mockery, or penalty.
  • Neutral Territory: Group meetings and foundation-designated areas shall serve as safe zones focused entirely on constructive teamwork and mutual support.

Article VIII: Amendments & Ratification

  • Charter Revisions: Proposed updates to these rules must be submitted in writing and ratified by a two-thirds vote of the General Assembly.
  • Faculty Oversight: All amendments must align with official school policy and receive final approval from the faculty advisor.
